\textit{Research Assistant} \hfill Sep 2025 -- Present
\begin{itemize}
\item Lead analyst for a GPS-collar study of cattle resource selection during dormant-season

  topography, distance to water and perennial forage; validated with animal-grouped
  cross-validation and the Boyce index
\item Built a reproducible R pipeline from raw telemetry to validated models and selection maps
\item Collected vegetation data for the Sagebrush Steppe Treatment Evaluation Project (SageSTEP) and
  a targeted sheep-grazing study (summer 2026): line-point intercept (BLM AIM), canopy and basal
  gap, density, biomass, shrub volume and soil stability
